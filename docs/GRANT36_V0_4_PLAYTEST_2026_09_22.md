## Human design conclusion

Light selection and movable shutter selection become cumbersome when they are central unknowns, especially when both are present.

They are not deleted from SHADOW SUM. Existing stages that felt good may remain. But new v0.5 replacement stages should not use free Light selection or a movable Shutter as their main source of difficulty.

Prefer difficulty that is visible on the board:

- placement geometry,
- missing sockets / board shape,
- Normal vs Tall,
- Flat Plate orientation,
- FOG as partial observation,
- overlaps and zeros.

The target direction for v0.5 is **BOARD SHAPES**.
