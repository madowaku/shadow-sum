# NOX character lock v0.1

NOX is one white dominant, black and gray tricolor cat. The three tones echo light, partial shadow, and shadow in NOXSUM. All poses, portraits, HOME art, and store art must remain recognizably the same cat.

## Required visual identifiers

| Region | Fixed pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Overall coat | Warm off white about 55–65%, neutral gray about 20–30%, soft black about 15–25% |
| Face | White central muzzle and blaze to the pink nose; black crown; gray on one side; calm muted yellow green eyes |
| Ears | Large natural ears, dark outside and pale pink inside |
| Back | White ground around one large upper black patch and visible gray patches |
| Flank and haunch | One distinct medium gray island; white remains the dominant ground |
| Chest, belly, legs | Mostly white |
| Tail | Thick, fluffy, dark gray to black, dark tip |
| Collar | Black collar, small antique gold charm |

The face has natural asymmetry. The viewer's left reads a little lighter; the viewer's right carries more gray and black. The white center still reaches the nose. The cat looks quietly observant, with no exaggerated expression.

## Minimum read at game size

Keep the white body, one black back patch, one gray flank or haunch patch, and the dark tail. SIT must preserve the white facial center. STAND must show the white body between dark patches. WALK must show the black back, gray waist, and dark tail from the side. SLEEP must retain separate white, gray, and black masses in the curled silhouette. The WALK direction sprite shows the same cat from another angle, not a recolored variant.

The HOME rear view must show black crown, white back surrounding black and gray patches, and the dark tail. The face may be in partial profile, but NOX looks outward through the window.

## Review rejection

Reject an image when the cat reads as black and white only, the gray becomes warm brown, the back becomes a mostly black saddle, the muzzle loses its white center, the tail is pale, or the patch arrangement shifts so far that it resembles another cat. Use these rules when generating or retouching future art.
